Title: Transcoding Queue Growth and Backlog Diagnosis
Type: Current operational runbook
Tags: transcoding, queue, backlog, worker-capacity

A rapidly increasing transcoding queue can indicate failed or slow workers. Queue growth is a symptom and should be correlated with worker errors, disk utilization, CPU, or other direct telemetry before identifying a root cause.

A queue increase alone is insufficient to prove why transcoding workers are failing.
