KB ID: KB-004
Title: Playback Manifest Not Found Troubleshooting
Type: Current operational runbook
Tags: playback, manifest, HTTP-503, transcoding

A missing manifest for a newly uploaded video can be a downstream symptom when the transcoding pipeline has not successfully produced the required playback artifacts. Inspect transcoding job status and logs before blaming playback-service itself.

HTTP 503 at playback does not by itself identify the upstream root cause.
