Title: Video Transcoding Temporary Workspace Exhaustion Runbook
Type: Current operational runbook
Tags: transcoding, FFmpeg, ENOSPC, temporary-workspace, queue-growth

When the transcoding service reports ENOSPC while writing FFmpeg temporary files, inspect node disk utilization and temporary workspace allocation. A workspace reduction can cause temporary files to exhaust available disk. Retained retry artifacts can accelerate exhaustion.

Typical causal pattern:
deployment/configuration change -> reduced workspace capacity or retained artifacts -> high disk utilization -> ENOSPC -> transcoding job failures -> queue growth -> downstream missing manifests.

This runbook is general guidance. It does not prove that a particular incident had this cause; incident telemetry must establish that connection.
